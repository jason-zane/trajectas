Three passes over the role, then the choice is yours.

First

It reads the role.

Title, level, function, the decisions the job owns, the context it sits in. What it understood is shown back to you, so a misread is caught early.

Then

It weighs every capability against it.

Each of the library's definitions is scored for how much this role turns on it, with the line of the description that says so.

Last

It pins the few that matter. You can move them.

Swap a close alternative in, take one out, and watch the length change. Then one click creates the assessment and sends you the link.

A report about the person, not a percentile.

Each capability comes back as a band with what it looks like at that level — strengths, the overuse risk, and what to ask about at interview. It arrives as a PDF and as a page.
